    T3 Authentic Thai & Tea

    Craving Thai flavors with a local twist? T3 Authentic Thai & Tea brings classic Thai dishes and refreshing Thai milk tea to Liliw, with Filipino-inspired touches woven into its menu and cozy space. Whether you’re catching up with friends or taking a breather, it’s a local spot worth discovering for a good sip, snack, and relaxing time.
