ARLENE’S BAYONG AND SOUVENIR SHOP

Hand-painted bayongs, keepsakes, and little finds from Liliw.

Operating Hours

Arlene’s Bayong and Souvenir Shop offers a variety of local finds, from bayongs and accessories to keychains, figurines, and rosaries. What makes the shop stand out are its hand-painted bayongs, giving a familiar everyday item a creative and personal touch. Whether you’re looking for a useful piece, a small keepsake, or a unique souvenir to bring home, there’s plenty to browse and discover.
