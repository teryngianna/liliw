VILSON started as a local business in Liliw and eventually expanded its collection to include bayongs and knitted bags. Today, these pieces give shoppers something different to browse, whether you’re looking for an everyday bag, a practical carry-all, or a locally made find to bring home. With its mix of useful and handmade pieces, VILSON is worth checking out when exploring Liliw’s local shops.
