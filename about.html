    LOVE, MADE LOCAL

    Liliw may be known for its footwear, but there is so much more to the town than that. Love, Made Local shines a light on the businesses, makers, and experiences that make Liliw unique. From food and handicrafts to hospitality and other growing MSMEs, it brings their stories and offerings together to make them easier to discover, support, and appreciate. At its heart, Love, Made Local celebrates the people behind Liliw’s local businesses and encourages everyone to look beyond what the town is known for and discover what else it has to offer.
